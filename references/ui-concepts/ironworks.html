Concept 1 — Ironworks

numbered tick boxes · day sections · level ladders
1 2 3
  • Number and tick are one box: order number by default, cobalt ✓ when done. Tap to toggle mid-session.
  • Top check + gear removed. Settings becomes the 5th tab; a day counts as done when its boxes are ticked.
  • Days get sections — MAIN, then OPTIONAL · REHAB — numbering continues straight through. Skip the optional block when recovery is bad.
  • Levels: exercises live on a movement ladder (push-up → incline DB → bench). Never on rows — the sheet shows the ladder with easier/harder direction, so you can de-escalate when fatigued or escalate when ready.
  • Muscles are numbered rows: Primary 1 Chest — upper, mid · Secondary 1 Triceps, 2 Shoulders — front. Heads are small add-on detail, sheet-only.
  • A program is just a group of days — the hairline in the tab strip is the boundary (gym block | home block).
  • Sets × reps get their own line under the name — mono, aligned, never ragged however long the prescription is.
