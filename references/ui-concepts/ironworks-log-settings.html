Training rules

Progression

  • Weeks 1–2: light weights, 3–4 RIR.
  • Add weight only when form and next-day response stay controlled.

Pain rule

  • 0–2/10: okay.
  • 3/10: reduce weight or range.
  • Sharp or pinchy pain: stop.

Unilateral rule

  • Start with the weaker side.
  • Match those reps with the stronger side.

Duplication rule

  • If rehab was done at the gym, skip the home copy or do one easy set.

Training rules. Owner-provided content remains readable and separate from exercise classification or automatic recommendations.
