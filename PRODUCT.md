# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person uses this app on their phone during gym and home workouts. The main job is to see the current routine quickly, open exercise references, mark completed sessions, and retain lightweight calendar notes without managing an account or online service.

## Product Purpose

Keep a personal gym and rehabilitation routine easy to follow and edit. Success means the workout plan is immediately usable during a session, completion history remains dependable on the device, and the code stays simple enough to expand without turning into a large application framework.

## Brand Personality

Practical, focused, and calm. The interface should feel athletic without becoming aggressive, gamified, or distracting.

## Anti-references

Avoid social-fitness feeds, competitive streak pressure, dense analytics dashboards, subscription-style onboarding, decorative marketing UI, and abstractions that make a small personal tool harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Put the current workout and next action first.
- Optimize for one-handed use between sets.
- Preserve local workout history visibly and predictably.
- Keep editing and recovery paths simple enough to trust.
- Add structure only when it makes future changes safer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specialized accommodation is currently required beyond comfortable touch targets, readable contrast and type sizes, clear non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, and basic keyboard and screen-reader semantics.
